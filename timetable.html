Term 4 Examinations Begin

Published: 15 November 2026

Please be advised that the Term 4 final examinations for Senzokuhle (Onverwag) Combined School will officially commence on Monday, the 23rd of November.

Important Instructions for Learners

  • All learners must be seated in their designated examination venues by 08:30 AM.
  • Full school uniform is strictly required. The Matric creamy-yellow jersey is permitted for Grade 12s.
  • No mobile phones or smartwatches are allowed in the examination halls.

National Senior Certificate (NSC) Examinations

Important Notice for the Grade 12 Class of 2026

The final National Senior Certificate (NSC) examinations for our Grade 12 learners will officially commence in late October. This is the culminating milestone of their high school journey at Senzokuhle Combined School.

NSC Examination Rules & Requirements

The Department of Basic Education (DBE) strictly enforces the following regulations for all matriculants:

  • Learners must present their official Smart ID card or green ID book alongside their examination admission letter at every session.
  • Candidates must be seated in the main examination center at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time.
  • Only transparent pencil cases and approved non-programmable calculators are permitted inside the venue.
