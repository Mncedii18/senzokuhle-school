Academic Phases

A structured educational journey from early childhood development to final matriculation.

Foundation Phase

Grades R - 3

Focuses on core fundamental skills: Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics, and Life Skills. We build a strong base for lifelong learning.

Intermediate Phase

Grades 4 - 6

Introduces a broader curriculum including Natural Sciences, Technology, and Social Sciences, helping learners transition into independent study habits.

Senior Phase

Grades 7 - 9

Prepares students for subject specialization. Adds Economic Management Sciences and Creative Arts to develop critical thinking and problem-solving.

FET Phase

Grades 10 - 12

The final stretch toward the National Senior Certificate. Students select specialized subjects aligning with their tertiary education and career goals.

Admissions

Registration for the upcoming academic year occurs between April and October. As a Quintile 2 public school, we are a proud no-fee institution.

Required Documentation

  • Learner's Unabridged Birth Certificate
  • Latest School Report Card
  • Parents'/Guardians' ID Documents
  • Proof of Residence
