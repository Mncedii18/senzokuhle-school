# Senzokuhle (Onverwag) Combined School Website

A modern, responsive, and accessible web portal for **Senzokuhle Combined School**, located in Rietvlei / Greytown Road, KwaZulu-Natal. Designed to elevate the school's digital presence, facilitate admissions, showcase academic progress, and provide easy access to school notices and resources for parents, learners, and the broader community.



## 📌 School Profile & Key Statistics

* **Motto:** Knowledge is Power | *Ulwazi Ngamandla*
* **Status:** Quintile 2 (No-Fee Public School)
* **Learner Count:** 500+ Learners
* **Teaching Staff:** 14 Educators & Dedicated Support Personnel
* **Grades Served:** Grade R to Grade 12 (Foundation, Intermediate, Senior, and FET Phases)
* **Brand Colors:** Maroon (`#800000`), School Yellow (`#FFD700`), Black (`#1A1A1A`), and Matriculant Cream (`#FFFDD0`)



## ✨ Features

- **Responsive Multi-Page Architecture:** Built with clean semantic HTML5 and CSS Grid/Flexbox, ensuring smooth navigation across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- **Interactive Academic Trend Graph:** Integrated combination chart (Bar + Line) visualizing 5-year Matric (NSC) overall pass rates and Bachelor pass trends.
- **Dynamic Notice Board & Dedicated Announcement Pages:** Linked news cards that seamlessly route users to standalone article landing pages (`timetable.html`, `admissions.html`).
- **Comprehensive Staff Directory:** Highlights educator profiles across all phases, including a dedicated Learner Support Officer (LSO) / Psychosocial Counselor card.
- **Official Uniform Policy Visualizer:** Color-coded guide for Boys, Girls, and Grade 12 Matriculants.
- **Functional Contact Form & Maps Integration:** Serverless form submission handling powered by **Web3Forms** paired with an embedded interactive **Google Maps** location frame.
