2027 Admissions Open

Published: 28 October 2026

Senzokuhle (Onverwag) Combined School is pleased to announce that learner registration for the 2027 academic year is now officially open. As a Quintile 2 public institution, we remain a proud no-fee school dedicated to providing quality education.

Registration Timeline

Applications will be accepted at the school administration office from April through October 2026. We encourage parents and guardians to apply early to secure a placement for their children.

Required Documentation

Please bring clear, certified copies of the following documents when submitting your application:

  • The Learner's Unabridged Birth Certificate
  • The Learner's Immunization Card (Clinic Card)
  • The Learner's Latest School Report (if transferring from another school)
  • Parents' or Guardians' Identity Documents
  • Proof of physical address in the Nyamvubu / Mooi River area

Application Forms

You may collect a physical application form from the main office during school hours, or download a digital copy below to print and fill out at home.
